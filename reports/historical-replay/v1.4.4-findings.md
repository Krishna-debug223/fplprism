# v1.4.4 playing-time anchor

The one-Gameweek forecast now puts 90% weight on the calibrated scoring model and 10% on two appearance points multiplied by the pre-match chance of playing. Blank Gameweeks stay at zero. The anchor is applied separately to each fixture; it uses no result from the target Gameweek. This tempers extreme scoring estimates without turning every projection into two points.

The comparison below scores one player result per Gameweek. The forecast pass saved and hashed estimates before the scoring pass loaded outcomes. The 2025–26 season was inspected during earlier development, so this is a retrospective check, not an untouched experiment. The 2026–27 archive currently covers only GW4–6. See [the replay method](README.md) for missing historical injury, lineup and market inputs.

The gain is small. The flat-two active-player hit rate still wins. In 2025–26 all-player MAE worsened by 0.0009 and the top-ten projected players averaged 4.754 actual points, down from 4.789; in the other two samples top-ten selection was unchanged or slightly better. Do not claim that v1.4.4 solves model accuracy or that the active-only hit rate beats two points. A forecast made before a match must include the possibility of zero minutes, while the active-only evaluation is selected after the match.

GW8 stays scored against its original v1.4.3 checkpoint. The new formula first applies to future GW9 forecasts and must be judged against that future frozen snapshot. No past lock is rewritten.
