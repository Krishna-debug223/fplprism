# v1.4.2 playing-time evidence check

Model v1.4.2 changes only the playing-time prior for a player with **zero minutes and zero starts after at least two completed club fixtures**, when no previous-season start-rate profile is available. The previous prior kept a reserve goalkeeper near a starting goalkeeper's chance for too long. Players with current-season appearances and players with a historical role profile retain the previous prior.

The same two-pass replay used for [v1.4.1](README.md) was run again. Forecasts were saved and hashed before the separate score pass loaded actual points. Gameweek totals, including double Gameweeks, were scored once per player. The data source is the [historical FPL archive](https://github.com/vaastav/Fantasy-Premier-League).

| Season | Player-GWs | All-player MAE, v1.4.1 → v1.4.2 | Appearance Brier, v1.4.1 → v1.4.2 | Active-player within ±2, v1.4.1 → v1.4.2 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2023–24¹ | 26,624 | 1.148 → 1.044 | 0.162 → 0.136 | 72.21% → 72.20% |
| 2024–25¹ | 25,184 | 1.208 → 1.121 | 0.159 → 0.137 | 73.95% → 73.94% |
| 2025–26 | 27,102 | 1.164 → 1.051 | 0.154 → 0.126 | 70.68% → 70.66% |
| 2026–27, GW4–6 | 1,980 | 1.519 → 1.207 | 0.188 → 0.132 | 70.10% → 69.99% |

Lower MAE and Brier are better. The 2025–26 all-player RMSE also improved from 2.051 to 2.030; the archived 2026–27 weeks improved from 2.310 to 2.236. Active-player MAE worsened slightly (2025–26: 1.963 → 1.968; 2026–27 GW4–6: 1.948 → 1.979), because a few players with no prior minutes made surprise appearances. The top ten projected players in each Gameweek had unchanged average actual points in both periods. This is an improvement in forecasting who will play and in the unconditional expected points across all players, **not** an improvement in the active-only within-two metric. A flat two-point guess still exceeds the model's active-only within-two rate.

¹ Older seasons have different FPL scoring rules and precede or overlap the model's calibration data. They are stress checks, not clean validation. The 2025–26 season had been inspected during model development; 2026–27 currently has just three Gameweeks in this archive. Historical roster metadata are reconstructed after the matches, and live injury, multi-season player-profile and sportsbook snapshots are unavailable. The new prior may underestimate a fit new signing or a returning player without a historical role profile; official availability still applies in the production model.

Reproduce with `npm run replay:compile`, then `REPLAY_SEASON=2025-26 npm run replay:forecast` and `REPLAY_SEASON=2025-26 npm run replay:score`.

I also tested a separate integer “typical score” trained on 2023–24 and 2024–25 to maximize within-two accuracy among players who appeared. It reached 77.74% in 2025–26 and 76.71% in the three archived 2026–27 weeks, compared with 77.17% and 76.38% for a constant two. Those gains are small, and its 2025–26 active-player MAE was worse than the current xPts forecast (2.000 versus 1.968). It is not replacing expected points or being advertised as an accuracy breakthrough. The [research comparison](calibration-research-1.4.2-older-training.json) keeps the tested alternatives visible.
